## Outcome and scope

A deliberate suitable route tap closes the replacement, including a later point on the same leg.

Forward-order validation, crossing occurrence choice, direct drawing and automatic final rejoin; remove Plot points, Done plotting, Continue plotting, Change return point and staged Finish here/Review legs gates.

**Exclusions:** Pilot flight departure/rejoin execution, new value controls and timed identification points.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Editable pencil entry permits short-tap departure and subsequent point placement without plotting buttons. Eligible deliberate route tap rejoins automatically, retains vertical rows, and crossing alone does nothing.
- [ ] Same-leg departure 30%/rejoin 80% is valid; equal, earlier and completed occurrences cannot rejoin.
- [ ] Coincident alternatives identify leg occurrence/fraction, and a chosen option is revalidated against current progress.
- [ ] Rejoin finishes drawing; subsequent original/free-map short taps preserve geometry without extension or another amendment. While drawing with departure, proposed/original overlap offers Edit / Rejoin; after rejoin proposed-leg taps only edit. Removed plotting/continuation/return controls are absent.
- [ ] No Finish here, Review legs, Pick a point or Inspect leg workflow gate remains in preparation.
