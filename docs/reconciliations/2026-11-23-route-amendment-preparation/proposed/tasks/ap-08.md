## Outcome and scope

Assisted preparation supplies editable estimates and duration-based minute marks without changing the operational Cockpit.

Captured-mode prefill/fallback, field provenance, selective recalculation after geometry change, and preparation-only minute/drift projection.

**Exclusions:** Flying-Assisted portion of #211, new estimator math, live weather, planned-wind selector and flight ruler.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Default Settings remains Manual; capture mode once per draft and display it. Later Settings changes affect the next draft only.
- [ ] Prefill available Assisted values, permit overrides and require pilot entry for unavailable required estimates.
- [ ] A time override changes minute positions from working duration; drift off leaves minute marks visible.
- [ ] Geometry corrections update only affected Assisted estimates and visibly mark updates; Undo restores previous values/provenance.
- [ ] Manual replacement legs have neither aid even after Check. Original-route aid behavior and regular operational controls remain unchanged.
