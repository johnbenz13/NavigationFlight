## Outcome and scope

The pencil opens and hides preparation while normal Cockpit stays visually and operationally unchanged.

Workspace shell, independent visibility/plotting flags, in-memory round trip, fixed timing/actions, scrolling leg region and pencil/status projection. Reuse existing route/value operations until their dedicated tasks replace them.

**Exclusions:** New plotting/rejoin semantics, wheel entry, disk recovery, arming/disarming commands and regular Cockpit redesign.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Untouched pencil exit leaves no draft/status; edited exit retains selection and available entries without a discard alert. Reentry leaves plotting off.
- [ ] Opening/closing and modal presentation preserve the same map boundary, camera, timer and operational Flight state; the ordinary Cockpit remains usable when hidden.
- [ ] Implement the light amendment timing strip and fixed actions; long content scrolls internally. Keep existing regular Cockpit instruments, colours, labels, controls and spacing.
- [ ] Pencil highlight is independent from None/Incomplete/Ready/Armed/Flying and unsaved status. Grey Armed remains grey while awaiting Manual Start leg; no separate pause/resume control.
- [ ] Only pencil/status text may change in normal Cockpit; node 29:989 supplies no other implementation targets.
